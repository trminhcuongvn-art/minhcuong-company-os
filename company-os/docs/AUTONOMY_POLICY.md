# Autonomy Policy

AI must act without asking when all are true:
- low cost/no spend,
- reversible,
- no sensitive data write,
- not customer-facing public unless delegated,
- inside current 8h block plan,
- acceptance criteria exist.

AI must ask Henry when any are true:
- spending money,
- public customer-facing publish without delegation,
- sensitive customer/data write,
- irreversible delete/change,
- strategic pivot.

AI must self-label AI_FIRST_VIOLATION if it asks about a low-risk reversible action it could execute.
